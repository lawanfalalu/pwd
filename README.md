## PWD (Persons With Disabilities) Projects

PWD projects are initiatives designed to improve accessibility, inclusion, and quality of life for persons with disabilities. These projects aim to remove barriers in education, communication, transportation, and digital systems.

## Objectives
* Promote equal access to services and opportunities.
* Develop assistive technologies for daily living.
* Improve accessibility in public and private spaces.
* Support independent living and social inclusion.

## Examples of PWD Projects
* Voice-assisted mobile applications (e.g., talking watches, screen readers)
* Braille-based input and output systems
* Wheelchair-friendly navigation systems
* Sign language recognition tools
* Accessible e-learning platforms

## Importance
PWD projects help bridge the gap between disabled and non-disabled users by ensuring equal participation in society. They also promote inclusive innovation and compliance with accessibility standards.

Impact
* Enhances independence for persons with disabilities.
* Improves access to education and employment.
* Encourages inclusive technology development.
* Supports Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), especially Goal 10 (Reduced Inequalities).
